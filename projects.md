Research Ready Intern - Google DeepMind (with University of Exeter)

Developed and evaluated methods to improve the quality and contextual accuracy of image captioning for figures within scientific papers. Worked with multimodal architectures to bridge the gap between visual (figures, graphs) and textual (paper content) information.
